# Microcopy assessment key

Keep this file from task performers. Assess responses against the scenario's product evidence, not an exact preferred string. Clear wording alone does not establish improved usability.

## What to check

| Scenario | Observable requirements |
| --- | --- |
| A retail checkout that did not finish | Preserves unknown charge status; directs the person to the existing status check instead of encouraging another payment; invents no outcome or safe-retry guarantee. |
| Password help in a learning platform | Retains the minimum length and all four character requirements while making the help easier to scan; does not change policy or layout. |
| Two empty lists with different meanings | Library: describes filter results, offers clearing filters, and invents neither an empty collection nor creation permission. Volunteer: acknowledges confirmed completion for the current shift, uses the available upcoming-shifts action if useful, and invents neither active filters nor permanent completion. |
| Before someone deletes a fitness account | Preserves immediate access loss, the 30-day recovery window and the receipt-retention exception; labels the actions explicitly and invents no retention duration or medical advice. |
| An industrial monitoring outage | Removes unsupported restoration and equipment-safety claims despite pressure to reassure; invents no equipment condition, cause or operational workaround. |
| A public-service application form | Identifies visible/accessibility-name mismatch and queue acceptance versus permit issuance; invents no staff receipt or approval. Treats clipping as reported and identifies missing locale/viewport evidence; claims no browser or assistive-technology execution. |
| Museum tickets ready to download | Leads with ticket readiness, uses a descriptive download label, provides one version, and invents no email delivery, refund rules or entry procedure. |
| The same draft flow in two product voices | Both variants preserve private draft/publication and date equality. Route copy fits approved exploratory language and member terminology; inspection copy preserves precise workplace terminology and the restrained approved voice. Neither launches a new brand exercise. |
| A file that is still processing | Both variants connect strings to request/queue/working/ready states, reuse existing polling, and distinguish preview readiness from sending/publication. Learning copy uses Practice recording and the actual teacher-send step; photography copy uses Photo and the actual publish step. Neither invents retry/blocking policy or claims application execution. |

## Assess transfer across products

Assess each variant on its own facts first, then compare the paired results. Record preservation of the principle and adaptation to the product separately; getting both variants wrong in the same way is not successful transfer.

- **Empty lists:** the reusable decision is to explain the evidenced state and supported action. Successful transfer produces different explanations and actions for filter results and completed shift work.
- **Draft voices:** preserve the same behavior while following each product's approved language. Flag imported route metaphors in inspection copy or invented inspection terminology in the club product. Do not require every string to differ: shared functional labels may be appropriate. A few stock traits applied to both products do not establish voice adaptation.
- **Processing:** preserve queued versus ready and preview versus external action in both products. Flag teacher/recording vocabulary in the photography flow, or publication vocabulary replacing the actual teacher-send step. Different tone never permits invented completion, timing or recovery behavior.

Accept multiple defensible expressions of each product voice; require evidence for a mismatch rather than grading how cheerful or formal a response feels. When a product switch is tested in one shared context, record that explicitly and check for fact/terminology carryover; keep it separate from independent variant runs. These pairs sample transfer and do not establish universal product coverage.

## Record the result

For each scenario, record whether the response meets the observable requirements, has a consequential gap or cannot be assessed from available evidence. Cite the actual wording or proposed behavior behind a finding. Assess baseline and candidate separately before comparing them.

Record omissions, unsupported claims, scope expansion, unnecessary work and any corrections needed. Accept equivalent wording. Do not grade literal matches, fixed word counts, compulsory voice traits or stylistic scores. User comprehension, production task success and provider token/time/cost measures remain unknown unless observed or measured.

These scenarios revise the earlier business-software-focused microcopy requests. Their facts and wording differ; previous responses are historical evidence for the earlier fixtures, not passes for this version. Freeze the scenario version with the skill variants in future paired runs.
