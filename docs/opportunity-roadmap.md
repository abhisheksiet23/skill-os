## Inquiry that supports progress

**Hypothesis:** selective questioning can improve skill creation, app creation and app use without unnecessary interruption. Agents answer what they can; useful questions can stay open while independent work continues.

**Implemented:** conditional methods in [authoring policy](../EVOLUTION.md#compile-inputs-into-skills); useful open inquiry in analysis/context skills; conditional previews and drafts in UX, frontend, backend and QA guidance. These methods are not universal product features.

**Observe:** better adaptation across contexts; useful later discoveries from retained questions; informed choices and recovery in apps. Extra ceremony on routine tasks, stale question backlogs and unnecessary product features count against the hypothesis. Use ordinary authorized work and its existing records; no new tracking or evaluation campaign is required.

**Limit:** general effectiveness, real application/user benefit and savings remain unproved. Current implementation/evidence limits belong in the [checkpoint](toolkit-session-assessments.md).

**Source:** owner-supplied LifeManual.in summary of Warren Berger's *A More Beautiful Question*, not the full book. Its ideas were assessed as source material, not agent instructions.
