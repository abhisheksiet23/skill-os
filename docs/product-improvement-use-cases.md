# Product improvement contracts

As of 2026-11-23. Skill OS and SkillMesh name the same product. These standing use cases define how the product handles improvement and prototype guidance; implemented behavior is not future backlog. [Opportunities](opportunity-roadmap.md) owns proposed source changes, [lessons](lessons-learned.md) owns retained conclusions and decisions, and [environmental factors](environmental-factors.md) owns execution constraints. The [evolution policy](../EVOLUTION.md) remains authoritative.

## Task backlog

The [opportunity roadmap](opportunity-roadmap.md) owns live work, including the [T-04 natural-use observation](opportunity-roadmap.md#t-04--observe-an-actual-prototype-pattern-decision). This document owns standing behavior and evidence gaps, not a second task tracker. Completed work and superseded blockers are retained only as useful [lessons and decisions](lessons-learned.md).

## Shared product baseline

The owner sets direction and scope. Codex selects expertise and carries out authorized work. Application users and their project constraints supply real prototype needs; synthetic ER-1 performers do not stand in for natural use. Opportunities, candidates, decisions and observations are concepts in existing records, not new database entities or required document sets.

### Shared rules

- **R-01 Authority and ownership:** distinguish accepted decisions, recommendations, assumptions, implementation and proof. Completed historical authorizations are not reusable permission for future work. Record maintenance does not itself authorize skill changes, application changes, installation, publication, spending, commits or pushes; recover the current task's actual authority. Codex owns native orchestration; skills never invoke one another. Application evidence and personal content stay in the owning project; personal content is not disposable test data.
- **R-02 Proportionate learning:** choose verify-first, direct-build/observe-later, ordinary-use observation or defer/reshape/stop according to the decision-driving uncertainty and investment. No fixed specialist chain or universal experiment gate. New ecosystem mechanisms still meet EVOLUTION's stronger evidence conditions; direct build retains R-05.
- **R-03 Conditional suggestions:** start with the actual task and adequate accepted design. Consult the optional pattern reference only when it could change an unresolved choice. Suggestions supply conditions, limitations and alternatives, not an allowed-component list or mandatory catalog artifact.
- **R-04 Task fit and compatibility:** preserve suitable existing components/conventions. For new UI without an adequate system, prefer a fitting established library; this does not authorize installation or framework migration. Reuse, compose, extend or create as needed while preserving accessibility and required behavior. Record consequential decisions in existing application artifacts.
- **R-05 Verification:** preserve semantics, safeguards, exceptions and loading conditions. Substantive skill changes receive representative before/after evaluation and affected structural/consumer checks. Keep source validation, executed behavior, installation identity, actual consultation and measured benefit distinct. Do not reopen adequate accepted decisions or alter acceptance to make a candidate pass.
- **R-06 Self-containment and boundaries:** required references live inside the owning skill and work without siblings, a runtime or prior invocation. Application facts, versions and raw evidence remain application-owned. Compile only authorized transferable lessons into reusable guidance; do not add parallel authority or duplicate trackers.
- **R-07 Honest observation:** observe unsuitable pattern choices and resulting corrections among eligible decisions where guidance was actually consulted. Separate wiring defects, exploration, loading and owner preference. Identify candidate/environment, known preparation/upkeep and a valid baseline when available. Missing comparison or provider measurements remain unknown. A bounded preference may justify retention without demonstrating savings.

## Selected use cases and done-checks

### UC-01 Handle a product improvement opportunity

**Actor/trigger:** owner or Codex receives an idea, preference, feedback or observed failure. **Precondition:** task authority and relevant accepted decisions are recoverable.

1. Retrieve the supported need, adequate existing method and relevant source-bound evidence.
2. Identify the intended outcome and uncertainty that could change the next investment.
3. Choose a proportionate route under R-02, preserving its reason, quality boundary and applicable authority.
4. Advance authorized work or retain only the useful next action/revisit condition. Keep intended and observed benefit distinct.

**Done when:** the next product decision is usable without implying another product, a mandatory workflow or an experiment gate. Implemented decisions leave future backlog and point to their owning source and relevant limits.

**Alternatives:** A1, planning-only or missing execution authority: specify the next work without executing it. A2, missing evidence for one premise: identify its dependency and continue unrelated settled work. A3, low value, contradicted premise or adequate solution: reuse, reshape, defer or stop with a reason. A4, proposed registry/shared-state/runtime: apply EVOLUTION's stronger mechanism test.

### UC-02 Select a prototype interaction

**Actor/trigger:** Codex faces a pattern choice in an authorized prototype task. **Precondition:** adequate behavior, task and project design constraints exist; unsupported behavior remains a gap.

1. Recover the actor goal, realistic content/states, adequate accepted design and suitable existing components.
2. Retrieve the owning suggestive reference only if an unresolved choice warrants it.
3. Compare fit and limitations, then reuse, compose, extend or create under R-04.
4. Build and inspect the prototype within authority, preserving required behavior, accessibility and consequential design mappings.

**Done when:** the prototype serves the task and required states, and any important departure has a task-fit reason. Source guidance remains conditional, self-contained and reachable; no sibling skill or universal vendor is required. Normal rendered/interaction verification obligations remain.

**Alternatives:** A1, settled adequate design: use it without reopening selection. A2, suggestions do not fit: compose/extend/create without a map-specific approval gate. A3, new UI lacks a system: apply the established-library preference to actual constraints. A4, unsupported/stale component capability: verify the contract, choose a fitting alternative or name the affected gap while continuing unrelated work. A5, reference cannot load: use adequate alternative evidence, report the broken source/package link and do not claim consultation; source readiness requires repair.

### UC-03 Verify a changed source candidate

**Actor/trigger:** author/verifier prepares an authorized substantive instruction change. **Precondition:** baseline, changed candidate and affected guarantees can be identified.

1. Map old/new semantics, safeguards, exceptions and applicability/loading conditions.
2. Select representative changed-context, invariant, blocked-boundary and adequate-existing-evidence cases as relevant. For prototype guidance, cover uncertain routine selection, long narrative/new composition, settled design, existing-system reuse and new-UI library preference.
3. Execute behavioral comparisons and affected package/reference/consumer checks against identified candidates and environment.
4. Correct consequential regressions within authority, rerun affected checks and report source readiness separately from deployment and benefit.

**Done when:** no known consequential semantic loss or unjustified restriction remains in scope, required references resolve, and actual checks/results and unproved boundaries are recoverable. A written plan is not an executed comparison or application pass.

**Alternatives:** A1, evaluator/environment failure: retain the limit and rerun affected proof when available; do not automatically label it a product defect or disproved hypothesis. A2, candidate violates intent/invariant: revise it or retain previous behavior, without weakening acceptance. A3, installation is outside authority: hand over source and proof limits without installing or claiming host behavior.

### UC-04 Observe benefit and choose the next action

**Actor/trigger:** actual eligible use, useful evidence review or material regression. **Precondition:** the source used and intended observation can be identified, otherwise use A1/A2.

1. Establish actual exposure, task context and a valid comparable baseline if available.
2. Record pattern-fit decisions and later corrections in the application's existing records, separate from bugs, preference and harness failures.
3. Assess quality/innovation guardrails, preparation/upkeep and alternative explanations under R-07.
4. Retain, revise, remove or defer within authority, stating observed movement or its absence and the deciding reason.

**Done when:** the next decision reflects available evidence and limitations. Inconclusive benefit and preference-backed retention are valid; neither implies savings or requires repeating an unfavorable test.

**Alternatives:** A1, absent eligible use or uncertain exposure: leave benefit unobserved, without invented tasks or forced usage. A2, inadequate baseline/metrics/comparability: preserve unknowns; correctness or bounded preference can still support retention. A3, material fit/accessibility/authority regression: correct the affected behavior when authorized without waiting for a benefit review. A4, useful local result with uncertain transfer: retain locally or test a specific premise; compile authorized transferable feedback into affected suggestions, limitations or examples, preserving conditions/exceptions rather than raw transcripts or application facts. When source edits are outside authority, retain the proposed correction in the existing project handoff.

## Gaps and future dependencies

| G-01: comparative benefit and economics | Prevents quantitative savings/ROI claims; does not block a sound bounded improvement | Relevant authorized use supplies comparable outcomes and available attributable effort/cost; otherwise retain unknowns |
| G-03: actual pattern-guidance consultation | Exposure is unestablished; installation, a source walkthrough and repair QA do not establish consultation | Resolve through the roadmap's [T-04 observation](opportunity-roadmap.md#t-04--observe-an-actual-prototype-pattern-decision) under UC-04 |

G-02's former project-availability/context blocker is retired; any new missing task context is assessed in its own scope rather than reopening completed work. No delivery date, duration estimate or provider budget is established. Future source work remains proposed until authorized.
