# Website Deliver

Move a reviewed candidate to its authorized destination, prove what is live and leave the owner able to operate it. Keep prepared, deployed and directly verified states separate throughout the handoff.

## Prepare the concrete release

Identify the exact candidate and current live baseline, destination/provider, review evidence, configuration, redirects and limitations. Accept adequate proof from any author without requiring a prior skill or phase ledger. Compare candidate, configuration and destination with the evidence before choosing remaining checks; reuse applicable proof and focus preparation on changed or unverified boundaries. Prelaunch evidence cannot replace live destination checks. Confirm accepted content decisions and who operates the site and receives enquiries.

For meaningful launches use [release guidance](references/release.md) to prepare deploy steps, domain implications, recovery and live checks for the chosen provider; use its operating-ownership diagnosis where ongoing actions could be orphaned. Honor existing publication authority. If it is absent, finish the reviewable candidate and release plan before requesting it. Reserved DNS decisions, purchases and messages retain their own authorization boundaries; launch work does not imply new accounts or unrelated infrastructure changes.

## Publish and verify the real destination

Deploy the reviewed candidate through the authorized mechanism. Inspect the result of uncertain operations before retrying. Check actual live pages/assets, navigation, indexing intent, redirects, responsive presentation and the primary conversion, including production configuration and authorized delivery tests. Diagnose failures and apply bounded, authorized recovery; do not treat a preview as proof of production behavior.

## Hand over the observed state

Lead with the actual launch state and its consequence for visitors/operators. Use concise labels for prepared, deployed and verified facts, with evidence links and actionable gaps rather than a deployment diary. Record revision, time, URL, live evidence, residual risks and recovery information; update the live baseline only with what shipped. Explain common content/asset edits and ownership of domains, hosting and enquiries without exposing secrets. Scale the handoff to the site.

Define meaningful measurable outcomes, separating visits, attempts, accepted submissions, receipt and qualified demand. Neither deployment nor a short before/after snapshot proves conversion improvement. Recurring monitoring and campaigns require their own scope.

Finish with verified status or the precise dependency preventing completion. Missing candidate proof may merit recommending `website-review`; explain the gap and let the agent choose. No other skill is required or invoked.
