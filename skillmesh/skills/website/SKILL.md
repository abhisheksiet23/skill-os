# Website

Keep the whole website coherent: a real offer, a clear buyer story, distinctive expression and a working route to a useful business outcome. This skill supplies whole-site judgment, not a router or mandatory sequence.

Inspect the company material, existing site/repository and actual objective. Establish the offer, audience, buying trigger, alternatives, evidence, intended conversion, operating owner and constraints. Distinguish creating a site from changing the business itself. Preserve valuable content, URLs, integrations and editing needs.

Find the most consequential uncertainty. A new company may need offer clarity; an established company may already have everything needed to implement. A contained correction does not need new positioning or a full prototype.

Make page purposes and the visitor journey coherent. Write real copy, establish a company-specific visual direction and inspect a representative responsive experience before multiplying pages. Judge buyer clarity, honest proof, visual craft, accessible interaction and operational readiness together. Framework choice is not art direction.

Never invent customer proof, claims, prices, guarantees, locations or delivery promises. Separate owner statements, external evidence, assumptions and permitted public content. Simulated forms and generated conceptual imagery must not masquerade as real delivery or documentary evidence.

Keep the site or concrete direction primary. Explain the central story and important page or journey choices with the buyer evidence, why they matter and the action they support; avoid a disconnected set of specialist summaries. Keep decisions, candidate evidence and live facts distinct in the project's existing records. Honor user delegation and existing approvals; pause only work dependent on a reserved decision. Preparation does not itself authorize publishing, spending or contacting people.
