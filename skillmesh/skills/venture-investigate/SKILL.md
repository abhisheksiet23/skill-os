# Venture Investigate

Help an idea earn the right to survive. Prioritize truth and useful learning over making the idea look successful or finishing a framework. Work iteratively with the owner; do not jump to a pitch deck.

Frame the proposed customer problem, solution and insight. Distinguish user, payer, beneficiary, current alternative and buying trigger. Clarify the owner's ambition and constraints: a sustainable business and a venture-scale investment are different outcomes.

Start with the cheapest evidence that could falsify the most consequential claim. Consider founder access, capabilities, capital and appetite early, without treating learnable or partnerable gaps as automatic rejection. Customer access is itself a testable assumption.

Use [investigation lenses](references/investigation.md) only to find important gaps, not as mandatory stages. Reassess after meaningful findings. A costly delivery model may matter before market sizing; an obvious feasibility constraint may matter before interviews.

Keep consequential claims explicitly known, supported, assumed, unknown or contradicted, with source, scope and limitations. Behavioral commitment, payment and repeat use usually support demand more strongly than praise or competitor presence; strength still depends on relevance and sampling. Public offers do not establish competitor profitability. Simulated economics are not evidence of sales.

Give a coherent recommendation: reject, park, reshape, test, build or scale, with evidence, uncertainties and what would change it. Separate business attractiveness from VC attractiveness. Do not claim product-market fit or investor readiness without corresponding evidence. Stop when further analysis is unlikely to change the decision, or propose a bounded next test.

Keep the case in the project's existing records; the skill holds the method, never the company's facts. Preserve assumptions and decisions through later work without copying whole transcripts.

## Optional expertise

`market-research`, `business-validate`, `business-pricing`, `business-model` and `business-challenge` may address specific gaps. Explain the unresolved question and useful context; never invoke them or prescribe a chain. The agent selects expertise and synthesizes the result. This skill works independently when no specialist is available or needed.
