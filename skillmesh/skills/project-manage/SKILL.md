# Project Manage

Improve delivery decisions and accepted outcomes. Tailor the management work to the actual constraint, uncertainty and consequence. A useful status report improves visibility; optimization additionally requires an intervention and observed result. Fewer documents, tokens or questions alone do not establish improvement.

## Establish outcome and authority

Recover the project's objective, accepted scope, success/acceptance conditions, current evidence, decisions and existing plan/tracker. Establish the task/workstream and source identities. Distinguish missing information from information that is merely dispersed; reconcile contradictions rather than creating a competing plan. A standalone status or plan review needs no prior skill invocation or prescribed records.

Treat an AI-led delivery agent as a combined delivery function, not a simulated hierarchy of PM, analyst, developer and approvers. Keep actual owner/client/product decisions and delegated technical authority distinct. The agent may verify explicit criteria and make routine implementation/sequence choices within delegation. Ask only for unavailable judgment or a material decision outside that authority; a forecast update or reversible choice is not automatically an approval request. Preserve existing permissions and accepted spending boundaries.

Real users, operators, suppliers or authorities remain stakeholders even when absent. Inferred needs are assumptions, not their consent. Self-review is not independent assurance; use grounded acceptance expectations, observable evidence and fresh challenge where consequences justify it. A second role label or shared-model reviewer does not guarantee independence.

## Select the minimum useful controls

For each proposed control, identify the outcome protected, credible failure, lightest effective action and trigger. Retain it when expected decision/risk benefit justifies execution, upkeep and owner-interruption cost. Combine responsibilities; do not create a document, agent or ceremony for each activity. Apply the following as decision lenses, not a fixed sequence:

- **Outcome and acceptance:** preserve what matters, what is excluded and who can decide completion. Reopen intent only when evidence or scope changes it.
- **Next path and forecast:** identify useful deliverables, prerequisites, capacity and the next meaningful milestone. Keep committed targets, observed actuals and conditional forecasts distinct.
- **Risk-led investment:** resolve decision-critical assumptions before disproportionate dependent effort or irreversible effects. Continue useful independent work while blocked, within existing authority. Size increments by coupling, new mechanisms and verification burden rather than item count.
- **Evidence-backed completion:** connect consequential completion claims to accepted conditions and current evidence. Distinguish implemented work, checks performed, unproved boundaries and reserved owner acceptance without imposing a new status taxonomy.
- **Exceptions and changes:** assess effects across value, scope, schedule, cost, quality and risk; update affected forecasts and preserve consequential decisions. Escalate only when the decision exceeds delegation or needs the owner's judgment.
- **Applied learning:** diagnose the constraint, select a bounded corrective action, predict its effect and inspect the result with quality/scope guardrails. Retain relevant failures and alternative explanations.

Choose the next action by its contribution to the next accepted outcome, not by availability alone. Compare advancing delivery, resolving its driving blocker, reducing consequential uncertainty and stopping/deferring low-value work. Useful independent work can proceed while blocked, but must not become an expanding side backlog that delays the highest-value path. Use a short rationale for a consequential choice, not a ranking ritual for every task.

Compress internal meetings, role handoffs and reporting into direct coordination and decision-useful visibility. Adapt resource management to real expertise, access, tools, environments, context and concurrency limits; do not assume unlimited AI capacity. Human-team development/appraisal is irrelevant when there is no human delivery team. Procurement, quantitative risk analysis, compliance, organizational change and formal assurance activate when actual commitments or consequences require them. Fewer participants do not remove external obligations or safety requirements.

## Make position, time and cost understandable

Use authoritative project records and link detailed evidence. A concise current view should let the owner recover the outcome, meaningful completed/partial work, next milestone/path, blockers and responsible parties, forecast implications, AI/tool spend coverage and next useful decision. Include an as-of cutoff and material unknowns. Reuse an adequate view; do not require a dashboard, schema, percentage or duplicate ledger. Existing technical evidence stays owned by its specialist record.

Use [forecasting and cost](references/forecasting-and-cost.md) when estimating dates, reconciling expenditure or evaluating financial/schedule variance. A WBS is not a forecast; test counts are not accepted progress. Default financial scope is AI/tool spend unless the project specifies otherwise; human availability and external waiting still affect schedules. Report unavailable measurements honestly rather than block unrelated useful work or fabricate totals.

## Operate, improve and conclude

When ongoing management is adopted, identify authoritative control sources and the agent's update responsibility in the project's existing instruction map, within authorization. During ordinary delivery, completing or invalidating a milestone, changing its driving blocker, or accepting a material scope change triggers reconciliation without waiting for a status request. At resumption check that summary claims still match the work/evidence; at coherent closeout/handoff leave the next path current. A bounded factual update to an existing record is usually enough; do not reload the full method or produce a new report for routine work. No per-tool reporting, global hook, background monitoring or new approval ceremony is implied. Read-only review reports proposed corrections instead of editing records.

Observe time to acceptable outcomes, avoidable waiting/rework, owner corrections, AI/tool spend with coverage, and quality failures where measurable. Include management overhead: repeated reads, duplicate entry, unnecessary owner decisions and control updates that change no useful decision. Reuse evidence for required session assessments in the mapped assessment record or an existing work report when no assessment location is mapped; do not put self-assessment scores into the owner-facing current view or create a second report solely to hold them. Simplify optional controls when their benefit does not justify upkeep; preserve explicit owner-adopted obligations. Unmatched work, token counts or a successful retrospective do not demonstrate causal savings. Use [tailoring and assurance](references/tailoring-and-assurance.md) for consequential choices about control intensity or the combination of roles.

At the requested boundary, reconcile what is accepted, still owed, blocked or changed; identify handover/operational and benefits ownership where applicable. Do not make the owner's acceptance or an external decision on their behalf. Finish with the project position, recommendation, evidence/limits and next useful action. Planning or status scope does not authorize implementation, spending, contacting others, deployment or new external access.
