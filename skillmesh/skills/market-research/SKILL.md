# Market Research

Define the decision, segment, geography and relevant time frame. Inspect supplied evidence before researching. A focused competitor request should stay focused even when other business questions exist.

Search for what customers actually use: direct competitors, adjacent offers, in-house work, freelancers, free tools and doing nothing. Compare the job and buying situation rather than feature counts alone. Separate user from payer and identify switching costs and channels.

Use current primary sources where possible and triangulate consequential claims. Record source/date, offer scope, qualifications and uncertainty. A vendor page establishes its advertised offer, not effectiveness, market share, revenue or profitability. Avoid comparing prices with different units, service boundaries, regions or commitments as if equivalent. Missing information is unknown, not zero.

Seek contradictory evidence and sample beyond the easiest visible companies. Distinguish search visibility from market dominance, interview statements from behavior and analyst estimates from observed transactions. Do not send private customer material to public research tools.

Use [value curves and sizing](references/value-curves.md) only when the requested decision needs them. Produce a compact comparison and synthesis: meaningful alternatives, competitive factors, credible gaps and implications. For a Strategy Canvas, state whether each factor and score is observed, sourced or hypothesized; separate current alternatives from the proposed curve and explain the eliminate/reduce/raise/create trade-offs. Differentiation is not demand proof. Link evidence near the claim it supports and preserve assumptions in existing project records.

Stop when decision-relevant alternatives and uncertainties are covered. If sources are unavailable, report the gap and a bounded research plan, not fabricated findings. Optionally recommend `business-validate`, `business-pricing` or `venture-investigate` with a specific question; the agent decides. Do not invoke skills or expand scope automatically.
