description: Design or assess cheap, falsifiable tests of customer problems, demand, willingness to pay and adoption. Use when a business or product hypothesis lacks behavioral evidence; skip feature implementation and settled mandatory work with no demand question.
---

# Business Validate

Find the consequential assumption, not the easiest experiment. Separate desirability, feasibility, economics and distribution risks. Existing owner knowledge and research help frame a test but do not automatically establish customer behavior.

State a falsifiable hypothesis: which segment will perform what observable action, under what conditions, and why. Identify the current workaround, buyer/user distinction and reachable sample. Lack of customer access is an assumption to address, not permission to invent interviews.

Choose the cheapest credible evidence that could change the decision: existing usage/support/sales records, interviews about recent actual behavior, observation, offer tests, concierge delivery, prototype tests or payments. Match the test to uncertainty; visual preference cannot validate willingness to pay, and polite interest is not a sale.

Before execution, record hypothesis, participants/recruitment, method, success/failure signal, threshold, time/cost bound and next action. Choose thresholds from the decision and sample limitations, not universal interview or conversion quotas. Distinguish planning from authorization to contact people, spend, publish or collect payments.

Assess results honestly: source, sample bias, observed versus stated behavior, commitments and limitations. Payment is stronger than praise for some demand questions but does not alone prove repeatability or profitable delivery. Do not move the goalposts after failure; record a changed hypothesis and new test separately.

Lead with the learning decision: stop, reshape, run a specific test or proceed within remaining uncertainty. Explain what observations mean for the hypothesis and next action; distinguish accepted decisions, assumptions and unverified interpretations. Preserve useful evidence and customer vocabulary in existing project records without inflating a small result into a report or brand exercise.

Stop at the learning decision. Optionally recommend `app-prototype`, `website-create`, `business-pricing` or `business-model` for a specific gap. Explain the need; the agent decides. Do not invoke skills or require prior invocations.
